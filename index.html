A few things to know

Curious? Good.

Who is AWS Community Day for?

Developers, architects, students, cloud enthusiasts and anyone curious about AWS. Whether you’re just starting or have been building for years, you’re welcome here.

When and where is the next event?

The date and venue for Victoria are coming soon. Confirmed details, tickets, speakers and the programme will be published here.

Who brings the day together?

Local community volunteers, as part of the Canadian Public Cloud Association community. It’s a day to learn from each other and make meaningful connections.

How can I help?

Attend an AWS Victoria meetup event to get to know the community and connect with the local leaders about getting involved. Calls for speakers and sponsors will be announced soon.
